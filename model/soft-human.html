How the motion works

A target pose drives damped angular springs. Lower stiffness gives the arms and head more lag and overshoot. Smooth skin blends across the joints.

A full active ragdoll adds rigid-body masses, joint limits, ground contact, balance and grabbing. Station activities use equipment contact targets. Scrub the timeline to inspect approach, interaction and release.
