# Game passenger library

The active crowd atlas is `passenger-approved.json`, generated by
`tools/bake-approved-passenger.mjs` from all 232 reviewed exports in
`model/public/models/`. The canonical catalogue is
`model/src/characters/library.js`, with ten hairstyles per sex defined in
`model/src/characters/hairstyles.js`. Male options include the original, short and
bald styles plus buzz, crew, crop, side part, swept, quiff and rounded styles.
The seven added male styles deform the original tousled locks with lower crowns,
different protrusion relief and fringe lengths. They retain the source topology
and head/neck weights; short and bald retain their approved smooth scalp.
Female options include bob, shoulder and long hair plus tapered, rounded, angled
and side-part bobs, a long bob, swept shoulder-length and swept long hair.
All eighteen backpack-compatible hairstyles have standard, small, 87.5% and 75%
packs; the two long styles support no bag and side bags. Each pack keeps its closed front against the coat and
its fitted strap anchors buried in the pack. Lower straps sweep smoothly into
the back instead of ending in a square turn. Small backpacks have a tapered
rounded crown, fuller base and shallower side profile. One hundred side-bag templates
add left/right shoulder totes, crossbody bags and hand-held purses across the
eligible hairstyles. Adults choose equally among 212 templates: shoulder bags
for both sexes, crossbody bags for males, and purses for females. Children choose
equally among 114 non-bald templates with no bag, a small backpack or a crossbody
bag. Twenty female crossbody templates are child-only. Side bags have their broad
faces along body depth: shoulder/crossbody bags rest between torso and carrying
arm, while purses follow the hand. Standing and all walks share the slight
outward carrying-arm pose and fitted shoulder crown. Crossbody straps fit across
their full width and keep front/rear rays aligned with the neckline until the
crown, preventing hidden shoulder sections. Free anchors retain their positions
when a fitting ray misses, and crown samples bridge neckline gaps smoothly.
Whole-ribbon bounds and moving triangle spans reject loose pieces below the bag.
Each exported entry carries
explicit adult/child eligibility; random appearance, the viewer and art captures
use the same pools. Stable identity gives a 20% child / 80% adult distribution,
independent of outfit, size and gait. Height varies from 80–120%; depth/width
vary independently from 90–110% for adults and 80–90% for children. Children
first use a 65% height / 70% depth/width base. Game variants use 70% High / 45% Low opacity.
Appearance does not consume the simulation RNG.

Regenerate at the root with Node 24+ and installed model/game dependencies:

```powershell
npm run bake:passengers
```

This regenerates the reviewed GLBs first. Do not bake the raw reconstruction:
it omits the silhouette cleanup, closed coat lining and fitted strap surfaces.
Never hand-edit the generated atlas. Original source:
`prototypes/trellis-passenger/passenger-b-walk-forward.glb`, SHA-256
`4bb4a7ded62d5e5eeb97dcee6d3add711631b9bcc15210444ea113381bd2ef4a`.
Each library entry records its exported GLB hash. Locally reconstructed/authored
assets must not be attributed as Quaternius CC0.
`passenger-clothing.test.mjs` checks the recorded hash against every clothing
export, including trousers and swapped tones, before the skirt geometry checks.
If exports change, rebuild with `npm run bake:passengers`; changing only stored
hashes would bypass the guarantee that the game uses the reviewed geometry.

21 flat grey palette stops retain material boundaries and outward winding.
The game materials blend at 70% High / 45% Low opacity without writing to the depth buffer.
The atlas has standing plus 24 frames each for slow/medium/fast walks. Speeds
0.9/1.15/1.4 m/s share 1.12 s cycles and strides 1.008/1.288/1.568 m. Fore/aft depth
scales both sim speed and stride; side-to-side width varies separately.
Queued/riding passengers idle; pause freezes gait.
Picking and first-person eye height follow stable identity and displayed scale.

Forty appended bagless entries add ten jacket-free clothing presets per sex in
both top/bottom grey arrangements. Six female presets use long or midi skirts;
the other fourteen use trousers. Each entry uses bob or original male hair and
is eligible for adults and children. The viewer can preview each outfit with
every age-eligible hairstyle. Original hair/bag entries retain their order.
`outfit-presets.js` owns the shared definitions, `outfits.js` preserves the source
torso/sleeves/lining and closes the top hem, and `skirt-motion.js` exports 24
clearance morphs per gait. The baker samples actual exported clips with
`getVertexPosition`, including those morphs; detached simplifier parts share the
source morph weights and morph trajectories participate in vertex identity.
Authored grey values are exact palette stops. No runtime cloth simulation or
per-passenger skeleton is introduced.
The game-only exterior pass removes garment inner walls/caps and covered thighs,
and trims waist supports under tops so they cannot show through the shared 70%
material. The closed source GLBs keep those supports for opaque rendering.

The 232 variants share 226,731 complete animated vertices. Deduplication requires
matching colour, position and normal in every pose. Shorter topologies pad with
degenerate triangles to 9,370 triangles per passenger; the reviewed closed coat
and connected strap surfaces need more geometry than the earlier single model.
Index/region textures wrap at 1,024 pixels; the pose atlas is 2,048 × 8,082.
Pose texture width doubles when needed to keep the row count below 8,192.
The first pose is absolute; later position/normal frames store exact signed
integer differences with `poseEncoding: "frame-delta"`. These buffers are
losslessly zlib-compressed and stored as base64 with `encoding: "zlib-base64"`.
The JSON is 42.7 MB; its decoded buffers occupy 183.2 MB.
`render/passengerAtlas.ts` inflates and restores frame deltas once before GPU
upload, preserving the same positions, normals, indices and palette regions; older absolute and raw-base64
atlases remain readable. `passenger-atlas` pins byte-exact signed overflow and
legacy restoration; `passenger-library` independently verifies zlib decoding.
The crowd keeps one visible instanced draw, with no
runtime GLB downloads or per-agent skeletons. Both 16/32-bit indices are supported.

### Crowd Low LoD

`lowIndices`, `lowCorners` and `lowTriangles` (plus `library[].lowTriangles`) are a
second index table over the *same* animated vertex pool (§9.3): the baker simplifies
each variant's standing topology toward a 1,400-triangle ceiling at a 3 % relative
error, keeps a triangle only where its three corners still reference baked vertices,
and pads every row to the widest one exactly as the full table does. A padded Low row
is 8,475 corners (2,825 triangles) against 28,110 corners (9,370), and the 232 variants
carry 401,778 rather than 1,308,230 triangles — 30 % of the stride, its vertices and
its triangle work, with no second pose set and no second bake of the walks.

`render/passengerModel.ts` uploads the table as one extra index texture and shares the
pose/normal/region atlases between both draws, so nothing but the geometry, the index
table and the per-instance level is duplicated. Each packed instance records the
storey it stands on in `passengerLevel` (the `storeyBand` the detail rule compares),
and `CrowdSystem` packs every drawn passenger into the draw its storey asks for: 高画质
draws the storey being edited full and a ghosted storey (显示其他层) reduced, 低画质
draws the whole crowd reduced, and 隐藏UI always restores full bodies. A crowd that
shares the edited storey is still one draw; only a ghosted storey's crowd adds a
second. Pose sampling, identity, the 70 % material and picking are identical.
`passenger-lod` pins the table, the per-instance attribution, pause and single
shared-atlas disposal. The generated Low rows are never hand-edited; regenerate them
with `npm run bake:passengers`.

Production builds deliver the packed bytes as parallel binary fetches via
`game/scripts/passenger-assets.ts`, avoiding base64 JavaScript parsing and decoding.
Buffers larger than 20 MiB are split and reassembled losslessly; each part has a
hashed asset URL so the complete catalogue fits
Cloudflare Workers' 25 MiB per-file limit. The build rejects any emitted asset
or JavaScript chunk at or above that limit. Development and Node tests read the original
baked JSON with native base64 decoding where available. `passenger-assets.test.mjs`
checks byte-exact delivery, failed-fetch rejection and the size guard.
The GPU retains positions as signed 16-bit integers and normals as signed 8-bit
integers; the shader applies their existing scales when sampling. This preserves
all baked values and reduces the two pose textures' memory by 62.5%.

`passenger-library.test.mjs` pins the explicit catalogue, balanced selection,
all walks for every variant, pack visibility, topology budgets and texture disposal.
`passenger-size.test.mjs` pins age distribution, model restrictions and separate
body/shadow dimensions. `passenger-view.test.mjs` retains flat boundaries, pause, identity, eye view and
picking. Browser checks separately validate the actual GPU shader/live crowd.

## Historical assets

`passenger-human.json` and `tools/bake-passengers.mjs` retain the older Quaternius
CC0 outfit experiment; they are not the active game crowd. Keep their attribution
separate from the reviewed library.

Author: **Quaternius**. Source models are dedicated under **CC0-1.0**:

- [Ultimate Modular Men](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html): `Casual_2.gltf`, `Casual_Hoodie.gltf`, `Suit.gltf`.
- [Ultimate Modular Women](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html): `Casual.gltf`.
- [CC0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Retrieved 2026-10-11. The author's Google Drive downloads were quota-limited,
so the unmodified glTF files were retrieved from the public
[FreeModels mirror](https://github.com/agentkaerf/FreeModels), under the matching
`Individual Characters/glTF` directories. Mirror tree revision: `db3df04d1e4714298a09510b26fb6de6645138a2`.

Source files include their own rigged
`Idle_Neutral` and `Walk` clips. No separate animation library is required.

To regenerate, download the four source glTF files into one folder and rename
them `shirt.gltf`, `hoodie.gltf`, `suit.gltf`, `casual-woman.gltf`, then run:

```powershell
node tools/bake-passengers.mjs '<source folder>'
```

`tools/passenger-hair.mjs` authors the hair geometry; the bake includes it in the
same draw and animation atlas as the body. The offline tool uses the game's installed Three.js GLTFLoader and
meshoptimizer 1.1.1 (MIT, installed by the locked Vite toolchain). Source archives,
an asset service and per-passenger skeletons are unnecessary at runtime. The
locally bundled derived asset is approximately 2.96 MB before gzip.
