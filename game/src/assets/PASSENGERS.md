# Game passenger library

The active crowd atlas is `passenger-approved.json`, generated by
`tools/bake-approved-passenger.mjs` from all 56 reviewed exports in
`model/public/models/`. The canonical catalogue is
`model/src/characters/library.js`: six male original/short/bald variants with or
without a backpack, female bob/shoulder with or without a backpack, and female
long hair without a backpack, plus five small-backpack variants for male
original/short/bald and female bob/shoulder hair. Ten additional standard-backpack variants use 87.5% and 75% sizes across
all five bagged hairstyles. Each keeps its closed front against the coat and
its fitted strap anchors buried in the pack. Lower straps sweep smoothly into
the back instead of ending in a square turn. Small backpacks have a tapered
rounded crown, fuller base and shallower side profile. Thirty side-bag templates
add left/right shoulder totes, crossbody bags and hand-held purses across the
eligible hairstyles. Adults choose equally among 50 templates: shoulder bags
for both sexes, crossbody bags for males, and purses for females. Children choose
equally among 19 non-bald templates with no bag, a small backpack or a crossbody
bag. Six female crossbody templates are child-only. Side bags have their broad
faces along body depth: shoulder/crossbody bags rest between torso and carrying
arm, while purses follow the hand. Standing and all walks share the slight
outward carrying-arm pose and fitted shoulder crown. Crossbody straps fit across
their full width and keep front/rear rays aligned with the neckline until the
crown, preventing hidden shoulder sections. Free anchors retain their positions
when a fitting ray misses, and crown samples bridge neckline gaps smoothly.
Whole-ribbon bounds and moving triangle spans reject loose pieces below the bag.
Each exported entry carries
explicit adult/child eligibility; random appearance, the viewer and art captures
use the same pools. Stable identity gives a 20% child / 80% adult distribution,
independent of outfit, size and gait. Height varies from 80–120%; depth/width
vary independently from 90–110% for adults and 80–90% for children. Children
first use a 65% height / 70% depth/width base. All game variants use 70% opacity.
Appearance does not consume the simulation RNG.

Regenerate at the root with Node 24+ and installed model/game dependencies:

```powershell
npm run bake:passengers
```

This regenerates the reviewed GLBs first. Do not bake the raw reconstruction:
it omits the silhouette cleanup, closed coat lining and fitted strap surfaces.
Never hand-edit the generated atlas. Original source:
`prototypes/trellis-passenger/passenger-b-walk-forward.glb`, SHA-256
`4bb4a7ded62d5e5eeb97dcee6d3add711631b9bcc15210444ea113381bd2ef4a`.
Each library entry records its exported GLB hash. Locally reconstructed/authored
assets must not be attributed as Quaternius CC0.

Twelve flat grey palette stops retain material boundaries and outward winding.
The shared game material blends at 70% opacity without writing to the depth buffer.
The atlas has standing plus 24 frames each for slow/medium/fast walks. Speeds
0.9/1.15/1.4 m/s share 1.12 s cycles and strides 1.008/1.288/1.568 m. Fore/aft depth
scales both sim speed and stride; side-to-side width varies separately.
Queued/riding passengers idle; pause freezes gait.
Picking and first-person eye height follow stable identity and displayed scale.

The 56 variants share 91,444 complete animated vertices. Deduplication requires
matching colour, position and normal in every pose. Shorter topologies pad with
degenerate triangles to 9,413 triangles per passenger; the reviewed closed coat
and connected strap surfaces need more geometry than the earlier single model.
Textures currently wrap at 1,024 pixels; the pose atlas is 6,519 rows. Pose texture
width doubles when needed to keep the row count below 8,192. Quantized JSON is
about 88.7 MB before compression. The crowd keeps one instanced draw, with no
runtime GLB downloads or per-agent skeletons. Both 16/32-bit indices are supported.

`passenger-library.test.mjs` pins the explicit catalogue, balanced selection,
all walks for every variant, pack visibility, topology budgets and texture disposal.
`passenger-size.test.mjs` pins age distribution, model restrictions and separate
body/shadow dimensions. `passenger-view.test.mjs` retains flat boundaries, pause, identity, eye view and
picking. Browser checks separately validate the actual GPU shader/live crowd.

## Historical assets

`passenger-human.json` and `tools/bake-passengers.mjs` retain the older Quaternius
CC0 outfit experiment; they are not the active game crowd. Keep their attribution
separate from the reviewed library.

Author: **Quaternius**. Source models are dedicated under **CC0-1.0**:

- [Ultimate Modular Men](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html): `Casual_2.gltf`, `Casual_Hoodie.gltf`, `Suit.gltf`.
- [Ultimate Modular Women](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html): `Casual.gltf`.
- [CC0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Retrieved 2026-10-11. The author's Google Drive downloads were quota-limited,
so the unmodified glTF files were retrieved from the public
[FreeModels mirror](https://github.com/agentkaerf/FreeModels), under the matching
`Individual Characters/glTF` directories. Mirror tree revision: `db3df04d1e4714298a09510b26fb6de6645138a2`.

Source files include their own rigged
`Idle_Neutral` and `Walk` clips. No separate animation library is required.

To regenerate, download the four source glTF files into one folder and rename
them `shirt.gltf`, `hoodie.gltf`, `suit.gltf`, `casual-woman.gltf`, then run:

```powershell
node tools/bake-passengers.mjs '<source folder>'
```

`tools/passenger-hair.mjs` authors the hair geometry; the bake includes it in the
same draw and animation atlas as the body. The offline tool uses the game's installed Three.js GLTFLoader and
meshoptimizer 1.1.1 (MIT, installed by the locked Vite toolchain). Source archives,
an asset service and per-passenger skeletons are unnecessary at runtime. The
locally bundled derived asset is approximately 2.96 MB before gzip.
